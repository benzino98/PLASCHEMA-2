## Product Requirements Document (PRD)

Version: 1.0

---

# Overview

The Plateau State Contributory Healthcare Management Agency (PLASCHEMA) requires a modern, secure, mobile-first digital platform that serves as both a public information portal.

The website should be world-class, comparable to leading government digital services and modern health insurance providers.

The design should communicate:

- Trust
- Transparency
- Accessibility
- Professionalism
- Simplicity

The platform must support multiple stakeholders including citizens, healthcare providers, employers, HMOs, government agencies, and internal staff.

---

# Design Philosophy

Use:

- Clean layouts
- Large typography
- Modern card-based UI
- Plenty of whitespace
- Rounded corners
- Soft shadows
- Beautiful gradients
- Accessible colors
- Mobile-first responsive design
- Smooth animations
- Excellent UX
- Modern icons

The experience should resemble a combination of:

- GOV.UK
- NHS UK
- Stripe
- Apple
- Modern SaaS dashboards
